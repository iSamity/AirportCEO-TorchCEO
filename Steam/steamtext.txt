[h3]Welcome[/h3]
TorchCEO adds an optional cursor flashlight for Airport CEO: a point light that follows your mouse on the active floor while you play. Handy for dark underground areas or whenever you want extra light on the build plane. The torch is off by default—toggle it with your shortcut (default Ctrl+T) or enable it in the mod configuration.

[h3]Description[/h3]
While enabled, a small point light tracks the cursor on the current floor. Intensity can be set separately for underground levels (Z below ground) and for surface / upper floors (set above-ground intensity to 0 to keep the torch off there). In-game only; uses your existing camera / floor logic so the light stays on the plane you are building on.

[h3]Features[/h3]
- Config: enable cursor flashlight (default: off)
- Toggle in-game with a keyboard shortcut (default Ctrl+T; configurable under Flashlight via [url=https://github.com/iSamity/AirportCEO-ShortcutCeo-V4]ShortcutCeo[/url])
- Independent intensity for below-ground vs at/above ground
- Depends on [url=https://github.com/iSamity/AirportCEO-ShortcutCeo-V4]ShortcutCeo[/url] for the configurable shortcut (install alongside this mod)
